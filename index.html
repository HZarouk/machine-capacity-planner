Calculation logic
Net available hours/machine/day = shifts × (hours/shift − non-production minutes/60)
Required machine-hours/day = target demand × cycle time ÷ 3600 ÷ OEE
Capacity/machine/day = net available hours × 3600 ÷ cycle time × OEE
Required machines = ceiling(required machine-hours ÷ net available hours/machine/day)
Operators across shifts = required machines × operators/machine × shifts

Important limitations: This is a preliminary estimate, not a validated capacity study. It assumes identical machines, stable cycle time and OEE, one unit per cycle, and dedicated operators. It does not include product mix, changeovers, parallel cavities, bottlenecks, absenteeism, or shared-operator work content. Operator total assumes the same operator coverage on every shift. Validate assumptions before operational or investment decisions.
